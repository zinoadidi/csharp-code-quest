Before Week 1

Get set up for C# Code Quest

Please do this before Level 1. It takes about ten minutes. There's nothing to install and no account to create — it runs in your browser and saves your progress on this device.

01 Create your save

  1. Open the quest. Go to the link below on the device you'll practice on most.
  2. Enter your name Type it at the "Welcome, adventurer!" prompt. It's just a label for your save — there's no password.
  3. Start Level 1. Your points, streak, and progress save automatically to this browser as you go.
https://zinoadidi.github.io/csharp-code-quest/

02 Add it to your home screen

Optional. This adds an icon so you can open the app directly, without opening your browser first. Do it on whichever device you'll practice on — phone, laptop, or both.

📱 Phone

iPhone · Safari
  1. Open the link in Safari (not Chrome — iOS requires Safari for this)
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an arrow)
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen → Add
Android · Chrome
  1. Open the link in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen)

💻 Laptop

Chrome / Edge
  1. Open the link
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar (a monitor with a ↓)
  3. Click Install
Mac · Safari
  1. Open the link
  2. File menu → Add to Dock
  3. (Older macOS: just bookmark the tab instead)

03 Your 4-week plan

12 levels, 3 per week, Capstone in week 4. Plan for about 4–5 hours a week — either three 90-minute sessions, or roughly 40 minutes most days.

WeekLevels~Hours
Week 1 1 · Fundamentals & First Wins 2 · Operators & Expressions 3 · Control Flow ~4.5h
Week 2 4 · Loops 5 · Arrays & Collections I 6 · Methods & Functions ~4.5h
Week 3 7 · Strings & Text Processing 8 · Classes & Objects I 9 · Classes & Objects II ~4.5h
Week 4 10 · Collections & LINQ 11 · Error Handling & Robustness 12 · Capstone ~5h
⏱️ If you get behind, keep going. Every level you reach stays unlocked, hints are always available, and failed attempts don't count against you — retry as many times as you need. Finish the Capstone to get your certificate.
Questions? Ask in class. Otherwise, start Level 1 whenever you're ready.
